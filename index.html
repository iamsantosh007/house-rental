Ghar Khata

An offline Android app for running a rented house or chawl.

What it does

Ghar Khata keeps a landlord's rent book on the phone instead of on paper: which room is occupied by whom, what rent is due this month, who has paid and who has not, and what a tenant is owed back when they move out.

Main features

  • Rooms listed by floor, each with its own default rent
  • Tenants with their contact and identity details, and their move-in date
  • Rooms assigned and vacated, with the security deposit settled on the way out
  • Monthly rent charged automatically, so nothing is missed for a quiet month
  • Collections recorded in part or in full, with a receipt to hand over
  • A running ledger per tenant, and a statement that can be shared or printed as a PDF
  • Monthly reports of what was collected and what is still outstanding

Where the data lives

Everything stays in the app's own storage on the phone. There is no account to create and no server holding your records. Because a lost or wiped phone would take the rent book with it, the app can copy its records to your own Google Drive, and can also copy them out as a file of text you can keep wherever you like.

See the privacy policy for what the app does and does not do with your information.

This app is built for private use and is not distributed publicly.
