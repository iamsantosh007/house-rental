Privacy Policy

Ghar Khata — last updated 16 September 2026

The short version

This app does not collect anything. Your records stay on your phone, and the only place they are ever copied to is a Google Drive account you choose yourself. Nothing is sent to the developer, and nothing is shared with anyone else.

What the app stores

The app keeps the records you enter — rooms, tenants, who occupies which room, rent charged, payments collected, receipts and the settlement worked out when a tenant moves out — in the app's private storage on your device. It is not readable by other apps.

Those records include details about your tenants that they gave you: their name, phone number, address and identity number. The app holds that information only so it can show you your own rent book. It never sends it anywhere on its own.

Google Drive backup

Backing up to Google Drive is optional and off until you connect an account. If you turn it on:

  • The app asks only for the drive.file permission, which lets it see and manage only the files it creates itself. It cannot read, list or touch anything else in your Drive.
  • It uploads a copy of your records to your own Drive, one file per day, keeping the ten most recent copies and deleting older ones. Those files belong to you and count against your own Google storage.
  • Your Google account address is stored on the device so the app can show which account backups go to. Disconnecting removes it.

The developer has no access to your Google account, your Drive, or the backups.

Sharing that you start yourself

A receipt or a tenant statement can be sent out as a PDF, and a copy of your records can be put on the clipboard as text. These only happen when you tap the button for them, and where the file or text then goes — WhatsApp, a message, an email, a printer — is your choice, made in the system's own share sheet. Anything you send that way leaves the app and is covered by whatever you sent it through.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics, tracking or advertising
  • No accounts, sign-ups or passwords
  • No selling or sharing of information with third parties
  • No server run by the developer — there is nowhere else for data to go

Deleting your information

Uninstalling the app removes the rent book from the device. Backups already in your Google Drive stay there until you delete them, which you can do yourself from Drive at any time. Disconnecting the account on the Backup & Restore screen stops any further copies being made.

Permissions

Beyond the optional Google Drive permission above, the app uses internet access only to reach Google Drive when backing up, and the system share sheet when you choose to send a receipt, a statement or a backup somewhere yourself.
